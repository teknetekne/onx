Entrepreneurship Program Personal Data Disclosure

Last updated: August 11, 2026

This Disclosure Text has been prepared in accordance with Article 10 of the Law on the Protection of Personal Data No. 6698 ("KVKK") to provide information about personal data submitted to the Bank via email or ongoing communication channels within the scope of the ONx Ventures entrepreneurship ecosystem.

Data Controller

The data controller is Burgan Bank A.Ş. Address: Maslak Mah. Eski Büyükdere Cad. No:13, 34485 Sarıyer / Istanbul.

Processed Personal Data

Identity and contact information; the startup/institution you work for, title and professional experience information; application information regarding the startup, product, team, and business model; correspondence, meeting notes, and information contained in email attachments may be processed if submitted by you.

Please do not send sensitive personal data, financial account information, or data belonging to third parties that you are not authorized to share, which are not necessary for evaluation.

Purposes of Processing

  • Receiving your communication request, verifying your identity and authorization, communicating with you,
  • Pre-evaluating the potential investment, pilot study, or collaboration request of the startup, conducting necessary meetings and reviews,
  • Carrying out evaluation, reporting, and decision processes; ensuring process records and information security,
  • Fulfilling legal obligations and establishing, exercising, or protecting a right.

Method of Collection and Legal Grounds

Personal data is collected electronically or by non-automated means through emails, attachments, interviews, and ongoing correspondence sent by you or the authorized representative of the startup. The data is processed based on the legal grounds of being directly related to the establishment or performance of a contract, the data controller fulfilling its legal obligation, the establishment/exercise/protection of a right, and the legitimate interests of the Bank, provided that it does not harm your fundamental rights, under the second paragraph of Article 5 of the KVKK. If a separate activity requiring explicit consent occurs, your consent will be requested separately.

Transfer

Personal data may be transferred, limited to the above purposes, to authorized units and employees of the Bank, committee members or consultants involved in the evaluation process, the Bank's subsidiaries and affiliates, IT/email infrastructure and professional service providers under confidentiality obligations, and legally authorized public institutions and judicial authorities; in accordance with the conditions in Articles 8 and 9 of the KVKK.

Storage

The data is stored for the period necessary for evaluation and communication purposes and throughout the legislation and statute of limitations periods the Bank is subject to; it is deleted, destroyed, or anonymized in accordance with the relevant retention and destruction policies when the reasons for processing cease to exist.

Your Rights under the KVKK

Under Article 11 of the KVKK, you have the right to learn whether your personal data is processed, request information if processed, learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used accordingly, know the third parties to whom it is transferred, request correction of incomplete or incorrect data and its deletion/destruction if the conditions are met, request notification of these operations to transferred third parties, object to an outcome against you arising from analysis exclusively by automated systems, and claim compensation for damages due to unlawful processing.

You can submit your applications, along with information suitable for verifying your identity and request, using the methods explained in the Bank's Data Subject Application Form.
