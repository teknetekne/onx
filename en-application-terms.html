Application and Evaluation Terms

Last updated: August 11, 2026

These terms apply to potential investment and collaboration requests submitted to ONx Ventures via email or ongoing communication channels.

Pre-evaluation

The submitted information is reviewed for the purpose of pre-evaluating startups in terms of potential investment, pilot study, and collaboration opportunities. Receiving the application or initiating the evaluation process does not mean that the application is accepted or will result positively.

Non-commitment

Making an application, sending an email, holding a meeting, or proceeding to the evaluation process does not create any investment, collaboration, or financing commitment on the part of ONx Ventures, Burgan Bank A.Ş., its subsidiaries, affiliates, or any fund.

Role of Committees

The Evaluation Committee contributes to the pre-evaluation of startups and is separate from the fund investment committees that make investment decisions. The individuals in the Evaluation Committee do not have to represent the investment committee of any fund. Final decisions are subject to the decision and approval processes of the relevant fund or authorized institution, if any.

Personal Data

The Entrepreneurship Program Personal Data Disclosure applies regarding the personal data processed during the communication and evaluation process.

ONx Ventures is an innovative startup ecosystem that brings investment, collaboration, and internal innovation under the same roof.
