Cookie Notice

Last updated: August 11, 2026

This text has been prepared to provide information about the cookie and similar browser technologies used on the onx.ventures website by the data controller Burgan Bank A.Ş., within the scope of Article 10 of the Law on the Protection of Personal Data No. 6698.

What is a cookie?

Cookies are small text files stored on your browser by the website you visit. Session or local storage areas of the browser can also be used for similar purposes.

Technology used on this site

The current version of the site does not use HTTP cookies or similar browser storage technology; it does not run advertising, marketing, or visitor analytics. Therefore, since there is no non-essential preference to manage, a cookie management panel is not additionally provided.

Third-party resources

The Outfit font used on the pages is loaded via Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com), and interface icons are loaded via unpkg (unpkg.com). These requests are not made for cookie purposes; however, standard technical connection information required to transmit the resources to your device may be obtained by the respective service providers. This information is processed limited to the purposes of transmitting the resource and ensuring its security.

Legal grounds and transfer

The technical connection information generated during third-party resource requests may be transferred to resource providers to present the relevant content; the transfer processes are carried out in accordance with the conditions in Articles 8 and 9 of the KVKK.

Managing your preferences

There is no cookie or browser storage preference you need to manage on the site. From your browser's general settings, you can view, block, or delete cookies and site data belonging to other sites.

Changes

If any analytical, advertising, marketing, or other technology requiring explicit consent begins to be used in the future, this text and the cookie inventory will be updated before application; if necessary, a preference mechanism will be provided with non-essential technologies turned off by default.

Your Rights and Contact

You can submit your applications regarding your rights under Article 11 of the KVKK using the methods explained in the Bank's Data Subject Application Form. For general privacy practices, you can review the Privacy Policy.
